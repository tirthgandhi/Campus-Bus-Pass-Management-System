# 🚌 Campus Bus Pass Management System

A web-based Smart University Transport Management System designed to digitize and simplify university transportation services. The system enables students to register for transport, allows transport administrators to manage buses and drivers, and provides drivers with a secure platform to verify students and handle emergency situations efficiently.

---

# 📖 Project Overview

The Campus Bus Pass Transport Management System replaces manual bus pass verification and attendance with a digital platform.

Students register using their official college email, submit transport requests, and receive a permanent Bus Pass ID after approval by the Transport Admin.

Drivers verify students during boarding, while Transport Admins manage buses, routes, drivers, and emergency transfers.

The system also supports real-world scenarios such as:

- Driver replacement
- Bus breakdowns
- Student transfer to another bus
- Live passenger counting
- Digital attendance
- Notifications

---

# 🚀 Problem Statement

Many universities still manage transportation manually, leading to:

- Unauthorized student boarding
- Manual attendance records
- Difficulty managing buses and drivers
- No real-time passenger information
- Poor handling of emergency situations
- Time-consuming report generation

Our project aims to solve these problems through a centralized web-based system.

# 🚨 Emergency Student Transfer

The system supports emergency transfers in real-world situations.

### Example

Bus 1 breaks down after picking up 10 students.

Remaining students are still waiting at pickup points.

The driver reports the breakdown.

The system allows:

- Transfer onboard students to Bus 2
- Transfer remaining pickup students to Bus 3
- Notify both drivers
- Update temporary bus assignments
- Prevent duplicate attendance
